Why this role

Newborn screening is signal turned into a healthy start.

Every screen is a lab result that has to be accurate, fast, and trusted, because a family's first days depend on it. That is the work I keep choosing: taking complex clinical and program data and making it reliable for the people it serves. My record is public-sector and mission-driven by design.
